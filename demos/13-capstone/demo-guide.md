# Demo: Capstone kick-off

**Duration:** 10 minutes
**Prerequisite:** `labs/13-capstone/README.md` open on screen. Optionally the worked example `solutions/13-capstone/` (show the structure only; do not reveal the code).

## Part 1: The brief (3 min)
Walk through the six activities and the time boxes. Stress: "A small, correct, well-documented answer beats an ambitious, unverified one."

## Part 2: Scoping a question live (4 min)
With the class, turn a vague question into a requirement using `requirement.md`:
- Vague: "How open are developing economies to trade?"
- Specific: "How did exports of goods and services as % of GDP (World Bank `NE.EXP.GNFS.ZS`) change between 2015 and 2023 for the lower middle income economies in our course dataset, and how does that compare with high income economies?"

Then show how you would ask your AI assistant for a **design**, not code:
```
I need to answer this question: <paste>. Propose a design for a small Python
solution: steps, functions, data sources, how to validate the numbers, and
what tests to write. Do not write code yet. List risks and assumptions.
```

## Part 3: Logistics (3 min)
- Individual or pairs; choose data sources now (cached data is fine).
- Checkpoints: requirement at +15 min, first data loaded at +45 min, tests at +2 h.
- Presentations: 5 minutes each, findings **and** AI lessons learned.

## Key message
Everything from the course, together: define the question, let AI accelerate the build, verify rigorously, and be able to explain every number.
